Inspect actual text before marking a normative finding, especially modal strength:
recommendations and obligations coexist. For a single requirement, report surrounding
traceability or ownership as unassessed when their records are unavailable. Identify
the defect's concrete consequence and request missing engineering values. An acceptable
statement need not be rewritten. Other editions or project tailoring need their own
criterion mapping; do not carry these locators across versions without verification.
